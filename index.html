    employment_years = customer_data['employment_duration']
    
    # Calculate base risk score (0-100 scale)

    income_adjustment = max(0, (40 - debt_to_income) / 40 * 20)

📊 Key Financial Metrics

Return on Equity (ROE)

$$ROE = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shareholders' Equity}} = \frac{\$15.6M}{\$125M} = 12.48\%$$

🎯 Target: 12% | Industry Average: 11.2%

Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)

$$CAR = \frac{\text{Tier 1 Capital} + \text{Tier 2 Capital}}{\text{Risk Weighted Assets}} = 16.2\%$$

🎯 Regulatory Minimum: 12% | Our Performance: 16.2%

Net Interest Margin (NIM)

$$NIM = \frac{\text{Interest Income} - \text{Interest Expense}}{\text{Average Earning Assets}} = 3.4\%$$

🎯 Previous Quarter: 3.1% | Improvement: +0.3%

✅ All metrics exceed regulatory requirements and industry benchmarks

These are our core financial health indicators. ROE shows excellent shareholder returns. CAR demonstrates strong capital position well above regulatory minimums. NIM improvement shows effective interest rate management. All metrics indicate robust financial health.
